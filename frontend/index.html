METHODS & SCOPE

Read the match. Check the source.

Runs are filtered by research lane, endpoint and the chosen gravity scope. Exact material matches are grouped ahead of general context. Each record is compared against one coherent condition phase.

Match
Every specified condition axis and requested flow-direction check passes the illustrative tolerances.
Analog
The required conditions are reported, but at least one differs from the scenario or requested protocol.
Incomplete
At least one required condition is missing or cannot be converted. A known matching field never fills an unknown one.

Illustrative search tolerances

O₂ ±2 pp · pressure ±5 kPa · gravity ±0.03 g · flow ±5 cm/s

These are search controls, never engineering thresholds. A documented microgravity regime may be mapped to 0 g for search, without claiming a measured residual acceleration. Oxygen values require a documented mole or volume basis.

Coverage and comparisons

Coverage uses 5 percentage-point oxygen bins and 10 kPa pressure bins. Each physical run counts at most once in a cell. Aggregate or unidentified observations are excluded. The default grid counts independently human-verified records; the optional source-checked preview is explicitly unreviewed.

Numerical outcome differences are calculated only when endpoint, definition, units, phase and protocol agree. Differences in material, geometry, gravity or flow remain visible as comparison limits.

Source and model disclosure

Curated public reports supplement the uploaded literature baseline. No October 28 PSI dataset package was supplied. PSI catalog entries are identified separately from downloaded report evidence. Abstracts are document context and never counted as executed runs.

The existing fine-tuned EmbeddingGemma 2 checkpoint supplies retrieval where its verified index is available. Actual retrieval mode is shown per request. Saved training scores use synthetic and unreviewed teacher questions; independent human evaluation is pending.

Gemini, when configured, drafts cited scope notes. Structural checks cannot establish scientific correctness; generated claims require human review. Without a valid response, a deterministic evidence-only briefing remains available.

Development used AI for code, source extraction assistance and original decorative chamber art. The art depicts a fictional chamber. It is not an image of a NASA experiment. All scientific values link to source records.
